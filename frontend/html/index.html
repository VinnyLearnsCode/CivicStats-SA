NATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Public data operating model

Boundary layer South Africa

Administrative data is loaded from geoBoundaries and remains read-only in this prototype.

Available data Stats SA, IEC & live sources

Census 2011/2022 household profiles and selected household-service indicators cover 213 municipalities. QLFS unemployment and GDP-R are provincial measures. Treasury finance and audit records, NASA POWER climate normals, World Bank national indicators and publication feeds load on demand through the hosted API.

Methodology Basic-services pilot

Municipal profiles show an equal-weighted Census 2022 average for three household service shares. It describes reported access, not overall council performance.

Data platform Source-linked and read-only

Public indicators are read-only. PostgreSQL stores reviewed imports and their audit trail; administrative import and approval actions remain protected and are not part of the public workspace.

DATA INTEGRITY

Verified coverage: Census profiles for 213 municipalities; provincial QLFS unemployment and GDP-R; live Treasury finance/audit; and NASA POWER, World Bank and publication feeds. Stats SA's 2011/2022 service-delivery report includes municipal water-interruption and basic-service access measures. A separate Treasury/OpenUp portal publishes named infrastructure projects, with its 2026 Q2 completeness checks in progress. Municipality-level labour and industry output and a verified full 2026 council-seat dataset are still unavailable here. Census interruption measures are historical household reports, not live reliability monitoring. The basic-services score is a bounded Census 2022 pilot; a broader CivicScore covering finance and governance is not yet calculated.
